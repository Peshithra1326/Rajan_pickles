Our Family Heritage

The story of our fish pickles began in 1897 on the coastal shores of the Indian Ocean. My great-grandfather, Nathaniel Fernandes, was a fisherman who developed a unique preservation technique combining local spices with the day's catch.

For five generations, our family has guarded these recipes, passing down not just ingredients but the philosophy that good food takes time. Each batch of our pickles is still made by hand, aged in traditional clay pots, and bottled only when the flavors have perfectly matured.

Today, we continue this legacy, bringing these authentic flavors from our family kitchen to your table, unchanged by time or technology.

The Traditional Process

Our pickles are crafted using methods unchanged for over a century, preserving both flavor and heritage.

Fresh Catch Selection

Each morning, we select the freshest catch from local fishermen who use traditional sustainable fishing methods. Only the finest fish make it to our pickling process.

Spice Grinding

Our spices are hand-selected, sun-dried, and ground in small batches using traditional stone grinders to release their essential oils and flavors.

Slow Marination

The prepared fish and spices are combined with our special vinegar blend and allowed to marinate for several days, allowing the flavors to meld perfectly.

Aging Process

Our pickles are aged in traditional clay pots for a minimum of three months, allowing the flavors to develop their characteristic depth and complexity.
